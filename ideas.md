## 2026-08-14

- **located posts + explorer** — the base for what Ash wants to build: posts of
  any medium (audio, video, stills, text) tagged with location, and an explorer
  for moving through them. Everything below specialises this.
  - **the actual app** (stated in the places discussion): a spatial database of
    conversations and information — "I'm fed up with potholes" (conversation),
    "there's a pothole on Acacia Road" (located fact), "we fixed 100 potholes
    this month" (institutional) — where people both add information and match
    existing information, in patchy-network environments (canvassing in the
    countryside). Plus **agentic interrogation**: during a voter conversation,
    an agent surfaces relevant points from the dataset in the background —
    and that too must work offline.
  - **walk documenter** — tracks a morning walk, capturing regularly spaced 360°
    stills from a connected spherical camera (Insta360 X5), later built into a
    single splat/mesh model of the route. A post whose medium is "reconstructed
    space". (Candidate first real app — would force the muon/apps grouping
    question.)
