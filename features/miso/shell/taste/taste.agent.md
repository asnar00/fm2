# taste — the aesthetic standard

*You are building or judging a visual surface of miso. These nine
principles are the standard; satisfy them before asking "is it good
enough" (agents.md 4a). Each was extracted from the shipped surface —
the precedent is the proof it works. An ask from ash may amend this
file; nothing else may.*

1. **Dark ground, quiet light.** Near-black with a faint cool tint —
   `#101012` (panel), `#121215` (pills), `#161619` (pages), `#1a1a1d`
   (buttons) — separated by 1px borders (`#202026`–`#3a3a3f`), never
   shadows or gradients. The only texture is the graph-paper dot
   ground. New surfaces pick from this family; nothing arrives white
   or bright except the primary action.

2. **Hierarchy is dimness, not size.** Text descends `#fff/#eee` (the
   thing itself) → `#c9c9d2` (prose) → `#9a9aa2` (purpose) → `#77777e`
   (ignorable), at 12–15px, emphasis by weight. Make a thing matter by
   quieting its neighbours, not by enlarging it.

3. **Colour is a word, not a decoration.** Every accent carries exactly
   one meaning and appears nowhere else: `#9db7d8` = chosen (ticks,
   chips); the blue pulse = a build is waiting; the parchment pulse
   (`#d9c9a4`) = a message for you. Adding a colour means adding a
   meaning. All accents are desaturated — dusty, never neon. Emoji
   render monochrome via filters; colour stays reserved for meaning.

4. **Rounded pill or soft card.** 999px full-round for small
   interactive things, 12–14px radii for cards and sheets, 4–9px for
   small controls, 1px borders throughout.

5. **Motion is quick or breathing — nothing between.** Transitions
   ~0.18s ease-out (the toolbar slide); ambient states breathe at 1.6s
   ease-in-out opacity (the pulses); background fades ~0.8s. Nothing
   bounces, spins, or grabs harder than a slow pulse. "Gentle" is the
   asker's own repeated word.

6. **The list grammar is law (#p39).** Anything list-shaped uses the
   `.crow` form: status where the number sits, title bold, description
   one tap away, no headers, no inline prose, no ceremony. New sections
   join existing boxes rather than founding new ones (the less-busy
   doctrine).

7. **Copy is plain, playful, short — the app's own word.** "do
   something" → **miso**. No "please", no "select an option", no
   explaining beside a thing that shows what it does. Microcopy needing
   a second sentence means the design is wrong.

8. **Exactly one channel speaks at a time.** A change announces itself
   in one place, through the calmest channel that reaches its person
   (`/attention`'s ladder), and silence is the interface's default
   state.

9. **The two standing "no"s (4a).** A filter working hard to correct an
   asset: choose a source that gives you what you want instead. A
   surface that ignores what the asker's own history says they like:
   fails review even when pretty.
